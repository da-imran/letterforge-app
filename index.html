Game Modes

Four ways to play.

Each mode changes the pressure. Same letters, different rules.

🧘
Normal
Untimed. 10 rounds, 3 letters. Pure vocabulary — find every word hiding in your hand.
10 Rounds
⏱
Time Attack
60-second TTL, 3 letters. Race the clock — bank points before time runs out.
60s TTL
❤️
Survival
5 lives, 4 letters. Bad words cost a life. Zero lives, game over.
5 Lives
⛓️
Chain
10 rounds, 2 letters. Each word must start with the previous word's last letter.
Letter-Link

How to Play

A round in four steps.

The same loop runs in every mode. The rules decide when it ends.

🎲
Get Letters
The server hands you a fresh set — 2, 3, or 4 letters depending on the mode.
✍️
Form a Word
Type a valid dictionary word using only the letters in your hand. Repeats are allowed.
⚡
Get Scored
New words earn 10–20 points by length. Duplicates still bank 5 points each.
🔁
Next Round
New letters arrive. Repeat until you hit the win or lose condition for the mode.
